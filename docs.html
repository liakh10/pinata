Pinata Cat, party rules

One cat, one stick, one piñata Everything else is inside it

01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Pinata Cat

A blindfolded orange cat with a striped stick and a green star piñata on a rope. Inside the piñata are ten legendary memes, cut out of paper. Three hits and they all fall out onto the floor. Then you hang a new one.

The cat is a photo, the piñata is a photo, the memes are paper cut-outs, the background is one flat colour. That is the whole look.

Character
A chunky orange tabby standing on two legs, red bandana over the eyes, white sneakers, never misses anyway
The piñata
A classic seven-point star in green tissue paper, the Robinhood colour, with magenta and orange tips
The format
One hero scene you hit with a button, then the paper memes on cards, then the wrapper with the contract
Chain
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

02 · MANIFESTWho is inside

ten memes, paper ├── Doge much inside, very out now ├── Pepe feels good to be out, for about a minute ├── Wojak was already sad in there ├── Trollface problem? ├── Cheems did not want to be hit, was hit ├── Gigachad took the hit, did not move ├── Grumpy Cat no ├── Nyan Cat left a rainbow on the way out ├── Stonks came out up, went down after └── Amogus was sus in there, still sus

03 · THE SWINGThe three hits

hits ├── one the piñata swings, confetti, something moved inside ├── two a crack, candy showing, you can hear them └── three it bursts, ten memes fly out with physics and land on the floor, the top half stays on the rope

After the third hit the button says hang a new one. It puts a fresh piñata on the rope and the floor is clean again.

04 · CONTROLSUsing the site

Hit
The pink HIT IT button, a click on the piñata itself, or Space and Enter. The cat winds up, swings, the screen shakes
Counter
Three dots under the headline fill in with each hit
Sound
Quiet until you tap. Your first click anywhere starts a short party loop
Below
The ten memes on paper cards, then the wrapper with the contract, BUY, X and this page

05 · TOKENToken

The token launches on Robinhood Chain through the Pons launchpad. The contract goes onto the wrapper at the bottom of the main page and here. Until then both say soon and the copy button says not yet.

06 · PRIVACYNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. The hits are counted in the page and forgotten when you leave, the memes are pictures, the confetti is a canvas.

Music
One CC0 party loop from OpenGameArt, plays after your first tap
Reduced motion
If your system asks for it, the swinging, the bobbing and the shake stop, the hits still count
Affiliation
None. Not the memes' authors, not a party supply store, not the cat's owner
